Privacy Policy

While It Ticks is designed to work without an account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Last updated: November 15, 2026

On your iPhone

While It Ticks stores game state, History, progress, and settings locally on your iPhone. Event alerts are scheduled locally.

When a physical Challenge is active, the app processes motion, orientation, step, and barometer readings on the device to verify the Challenge. Raw sensor readings are not saved in History or sent to the developer.

The app has no backend or cloud synchronization and does not send your game data to the developer.

Sharing and deletion

The app does not share data automatically. If you choose Share, sharing is initiated through the iOS share sheet.

Use Settings → Delete all data to remove local game data, preferences, and scheduled Event alerts from the current installation. You can change Notifications and Motion & Fitness access in iOS Settings.

Support

If you email whileitticks@outlook.com, the developer receives the address, message, and attachments you choose to send and uses them only to respond to your request.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations. You can request its deletion at the same address.

This website

These pages use GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, advertising, tracking code, or cookies. GitHub may process ordinary web request information under its GitHub General Privacy Statement.
